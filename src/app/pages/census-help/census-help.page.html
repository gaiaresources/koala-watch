Overview
A scat census is part of a systematic survey that is generally conducted at a defined survey site. The census method used in I Spy Koala is the Spot Assessment Technique (SAT; Phillips and Callaghan, 2011). To begin a scat census, go to the “Records” tab and select “Scat census”.
 Tree Surveys
Recording the trees surveyed during a scat census are part of the SAT method. When working on a census, you can create one or more tree sightings. Select the “Tree surveys” tab at the top of the page and use the “Create a tree survey for this census” button to create a new tree sighting. For each tree sighting, record the individual tree species that have been searched for koalas and their scats.
 Viewing records
Once you have recorded your data, a list of the records will be displayed in the “My records” page. You can switch to see the records displayed on a map view using the “Map” tab button.
Uploading records
When one of more records have been completed, you can upload them by clicking the “Upload my records” button in the “My records” page. Completed records (shown in blue) are those where all required fields are populated within the allowable constraints. You can upload your completed records even if some other records are incomplete (shown in red). Incomplete records will not be uploaded.
 GPS accuracy
The accuracy of the GPS in your device will improve over time after starting as it picks up signals from more satellites. You may wish to update the location information in your observation or census periodically if the current accuracy is insufficient. This can be done by pressing the “Use Current Location” button within a record form.
