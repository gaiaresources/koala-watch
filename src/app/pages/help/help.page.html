Recording observations
To get started, go to the “Records” tab and select the “Observation” button to record an observation. Record your observation when a koala or koala sign (call, scat etc) has been observed at a particular location. Remember to take a photo if possible. To learn more about a systematic Scat census (koala survey), go to the “Scat census” tab.
 Uploading Records
When one of more records have been completed, you can upload them to the server by clicking the “Upload” button. Completed records (shown in blue) are those where all required fields are populated within the allowable constraints. You can upload your completed records even if some other records are incomplete (shown in red). Incomplete records will not be uploaded.
 GPS accuracy
The accuracy of the GPS in your device will improve over time after starting as it picks up signals from more satellites. You may wish to update the location information in your observation or census periodically if the current accuracy is insufficient. This can be done by pressing the “Use Current Location” button within a record form.
